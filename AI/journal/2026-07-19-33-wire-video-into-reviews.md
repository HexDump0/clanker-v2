# Wire video generation into normal review runs

**Date:** 2026-07-19 · **Agent:** Codex (GPT-5) · **Type:** fix

## What was done
- Investigated Logfire trace `019f7a72d1f0d204bc1876a30ca3edbc` after the human
  reported that no video was generated.
- Confirmed the review returned two valid `video_evidence` items, but `ReviewRunner`
  stopped after PDF generation and never invoked the screenshot video pipeline.
- Wired `VisionDirector` into the application object graph and `generate_review_video`
  into `ReviewRunner` whenever video generation is enabled and evidence is present.
- Added `video_path` and `video_error` to `ReviewOutcome`; failures remain isolated from
  the verdict and PDF.
- Added video path/error to the Slack `run_review` tool response and CLI output.
- Added MP4 uploads to both watcher announcements and streamed Slack tool results.
- Added configuration for enabling video and choosing video/output work directories.
- Added integration tests for runner invocation and both Slack upload paths.

## Why / decisions made
- The earlier implementation was only callable as a standalone pipeline; describing it
  as fully generated by a normal review was inaccurate. Normal `run_review` calls now
  invoke it directly.
- No video is attempted when the authoritative review supplies no browser-visible
  evidence. Video failures are recorded but never invalidate the completed review/PDF.
- The running `clanker run` process predates this wiring and must be restarted before it
  can exercise the new code.
