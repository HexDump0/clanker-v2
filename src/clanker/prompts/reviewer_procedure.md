# Human Reviewer Procedure

These are reviewer obligations, not extra reasons to reject a ship. Clanker may gather
evidence and recommend a verdict, but it must never claim it completed a human action it
did not perform.

## Project page and banner

- Open the Stardance project page and confirm the banner is a screenshot of the project
  in action. It cannot be an AI-generated image, a logo, or something inappropriate or
  irrelevant to the project.
- Check that the devlogs appear to be written by the shipper rather than wholly AI-made.
- Never reject merely because this evidence was unavailable to Clanker. Surface the
  unresolved human check in the result instead.

## Functional testing

- Read the README, follow its install/download instructions, and test the project's
  described features.
- For projects with authentication, create a fresh account. Test at least one available
  OAuth option and the conventional sign-up/login flow, then test the authenticated
  features. A special shipwright/premade account is not acceptable.

## Required proof video

- The human reviewer must record a proof video showing the GitHub repository, reading
  the README, downloading/opening the project, and testing its features. Capture audio
  when sound is part of the project.
- Do **not** show the Shipwrights Dashboard in the recording.
- Do not use Google Drive for a required demo/proof video.
- Upload the proof video in the dashboard's Final Verdict section and leave polite,
  well-structured professional feedback explaining a rejection or offering useful notes
  on an approval.
- Clanker's generated issue-highlighting/walkthrough video is a separate advisory
  artifact and does **not** satisfy this proof-video requirement.
